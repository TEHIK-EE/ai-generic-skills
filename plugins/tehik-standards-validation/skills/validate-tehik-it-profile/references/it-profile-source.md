# IT-Profile EN

**Version: 3.0**

**General information technology profile of the administration and development of hardware and software and e-services in the Ministry of Social Affairs**

## General

1.  The goals of the information technology profile (hereafter IT profile) are:

    1.  describe the standard configurations of hardware and software managed by the Center for Health and Welfare Information Systems (hereinafter TEHIK) and their minimum parameters;
    2.  reduction of administrative, maintenance and training costs for the existing information system;
    3.  minimization of problems and costs that may arise through the integration of various parts of the information system;
    4.  guaranteeing continued development of all information technology (hereinafter IT) solutions used in the administrative area of the Ministry of Social Affairs;
    5.  Unity of the software and hardware used will result in achieving significant savings through centralized procurement;
    6.  minimization of security risks affecting the systems;
    7.  create an effective, safe and comfortable working environment for users;

2.  When selecting and implementing Off the Shelf Software, the IT profile must be applied as much as possible. Deviations from the IT profile are allowed if they are unavoidable and approved by the TEHIK architectural council;
3.  The IT profile is adjusted as needed, but at least once a year. The modified IT profile is approved by the TEHIK architectural council.

## Technical requirements for e-services based on information systems and infrastructure managed by TEHIK

1.  The principles of selection of IT products and components are:

    1.  with the same functionality, but the number of components from different manufacturers must be kept to a minimum. When purchasing standard hardware, it is advisable to prefer equipment from one manufacturer, which ensures better performance of the entire IT infrastructure and equipment compatibility;
    2.  all selected products must meet current standards, open standards must be preferred;
    3.  software in the test stage (beta, release candidate, etc.) may only be used for testing purposes;
    4.  components and products must meet the security rules set by the institution;
    5.  All components and products not mentioned require prior agreement with the TEHIK architectural council;

## Servers
1.  Hardware standard
    1.  X86-64 platvorm
    2.  Duplicated components (power supply units, fans, etc.)
    3.  Hot-swappable disks
    4.  Availability of a remote management interface with licensing of the necessary functions (removable media support, etc.), the remote management interface must work with modern web browsers without java/flash support)
    5.  The purchased hardware must have at least two independent (i.e. cannot be part of the same company/group) authorized  maintenance/warranty service providers
    6.  As a rule, the hardware is purchased with three years of NBD support with the requirement that the critical spare parts must be stored locally in Estonia. |                                                                                                                                      |                                                                                                                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
